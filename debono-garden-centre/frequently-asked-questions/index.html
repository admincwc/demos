Gardening Tips from Lidia

Advice from the family greenhouse for tending your beds, borders and containers.

Ask a grower

Have a question about your own garden? Ask Victor or Lidia when you visit.

How far apart should vegetable rows be?

Allow room to walk between root crops. For sprawling plants like cucumbers, leave at least two feet between rows.

When can I plant perennials?

Established perennials can generally go in from spring until fall. Ask at the greenhouse about timing for a particular variety.

How often should I water?

Check the soil with a finger. Water when it feels dry and adjust for rain and heat.

When should I cut back perennials?

Leave the tops over winter to protect the crowns. Trim them in late winter or early spring.

What soil works best in pots?

Use potting mix. Garden soil can contain enough clay to compact in a container.

Can I reuse potting soil?

Replenish it with compost and fertilizer before planting again.

How wide should garden paths be?

Leave three feet so you can still walk through after plants fill out.

What grows in shade?

Try hostas, bleeding hearts or painted ferns.

How can I keep grass out of a bed?

Maintain a cut edge or sink edging material along the border.

Which containers should I start with?

Larger pots offer more root space and dry out more slowly. Try peppers or compact determinate tomatoes.

How do I repair the lawn in spring?

Rake away debris, loosen bare patches, sow grass seed and water until the new grass takes hold.
