The Kunkel family business

Mike and Cheryl Kunkel own and operate Access Mechanical, a second-generation HVAC-R business in Simcoe, Ontario.

From appliance repairs
to commercial refrigeration

1969

Herm Kunkel Appliance Service

Herman and Sandra Kunkel opened their installation and repair business. Their name is still visible on the door of the service car in the family photograph.

2002

Access Mechanical is established

The next generation carried the family trade into commercial heating, cooling and refrigeration. Today Mike and Cheryl serve commercial, industrial, agricultural and institutional customers.

Our technicians are licensed and insured. With a G1 gas technician on staff, we can service gas equipment of any input capacity, alongside our refrigeration, ventilation and ice machine work.
