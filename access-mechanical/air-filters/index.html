Air filters for home & business

We carry wholesale and retail replacement filters for furnaces, humidifiers, rooftop units and make-up air units.

Call about a filter

Which filter do you need?

Check the dimensions printed on the edge of your existing filter. When you call, tell us the size, the type of unit and how many filters you need. If the label is hard to read, have the equipment make and model handy.

Homeowners can ask about a single replacement. Businesses and trade customers can enquire about wholesale quantities.

When to change a filter

At home, we suggest checking around the three-month mark and more often during heavy heating or cooling use. For commercial facilities, we recommend a change at least every six months. Follow the service interval for your equipment and site conditions.

A clean filter helps maintain airflow and keeps dust from building up inside the unit.
