Our Main Street salon

It started in 1984.

The Gay Blade Salon and Spa opened on Port Dover’s Main Street in 1984. When ownership changed in 2018, the salon took the name you see over the door today.

Our stylists make time for workshops with educators from across Canada and North America. Back in the salon, that training helps us choose a cut, colour or treatment for your hair.

There’s coffee, cappuccino or herbal tea on offer while you’re here.
