Behind the chairs

Meet the seven stylists and the esthetician at Blade Salon in Port Dover.

Laura

Master stylist

Laura has spent more than 20 years behind the chair and has built a loyal following in Port Dover.

Tammy

Master stylist

Precision cuts and colour are Tammy’s specialties, honed over nearly four decades of hairdressing.

Eddie

Master stylist

Eddie brings nearly 30 years of experience to the salon chair.

Peggy

Master stylist

Peggy is known for her easygoing manner and her eye for precision cuts and colour. She has 25 years of experience.

Holly

Hairstylist

Holly has been cutting and styling hair for more than 14 years. She enjoys finding a look that suits the person in her chair.

Stephanie

Stylist

Stephanie especially enjoys creative colour and cutting. She starts by listening to what you have in mind.

Alyssa

Stylist

Alyssa takes time with consultations and keeps up her education in cutting and styling.

Glenda

Esthetician

A Dermalogica skin therapist with more than 25 years of experience, Glenda looks after the salon’s esthetics clients.
