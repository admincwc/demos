Pickup FAQs

Check your reels and packaging before arranging a return.

How much material do I need for pickup?

Have at least four pallets or 100 reels ready before requesting a truck.

When will Murpac collect my reels?

Once you confirm the minimum quantity is ready, collection typically takes 8 to 10 business days.

Which reels and packaging can I return?

We accept identifiable plastic, steel and wood reels from Rea, Essex, Magnekon, Craigwire Products, Sumitomo, Alconex, Condumex and PPE/Invex. Associated branded wood or plastic pallets and end caps, as well as labelled containers, covers and hoods, can also be returned.

What should I leave out?

Leave out unmarked single-use plywood reels, unbranded lightweight plastic spools, loose waste and unrelated items. An unmarked pallet is suitable only as a base for stacking returnable reels.

What if I cannot identify a reel?

Contact Murpac with a description. A photo of the reel and any markings can help the team confirm whether it qualifies.
