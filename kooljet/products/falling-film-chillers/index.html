Falling-film chillers

Cool milk and other process liquids across a bank of chilled stainless steel plates.

Discuss this application

How it works

Product flows down the outside of an embossed plate pack while refrigerant or a secondary coolant flows inside. This arrangement can handle different viscosities and some suspended solids. Intermediate heat exchangers, pumps, valves and controls can be included with the refrigeration package.

System details

  • Accessible plate surfaces for cleaning
  • 304L, 316L and SMO 254 plate options
  • Available with an intermediate heat exchanger and pump
