Through-the-WALL systems

A one-piece system mounted in the cold room wall leaves the loading doors available.

Discuss this application

How it works

The unit fits into a prepared exterior wall opening. Once mounted and sealed, it connects to the electrical supply. Options include air or hot-gas defrost and an outdoor-air damper for facilities that can take advantage of cool ambient conditions.

System details

  • Available in capacities exceeding 50 compressor HP per unit
  • Low-ambient option for operation down to -20°F outdoors
  • Automatic air defrost or rapid hot-gas defrost
