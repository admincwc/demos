Private paint parties.

Have everyone over for a paint night. Pick a painting you love, set out the tables and chairs, and Lorraine will take it from there.

$45 per person · Minimum 5 guests

Ask about a date

Make it your own

Choose one of Lorraine’s designs or ask her to create one for your occasion. You can paint on canvas, wine glasses, water bottles, mirrors, hairbrushes or clips.

What to have ready

You supply the venue, tables and chairs. Lorraine travels to you with paint, brushes, aprons and table coverings. Everyone gets step-by-step guidance and takes their finished piece home.

Where you can host

A home, backyard or rented room can work. Lorraine travels in Southwestern Ontario. Include your town when you ask about a date so she can confirm travel.
