Bachelorette paint parties.

Give the bride a painting in her colours, with her name on it. Everyone paints a version of the same design and gathers for a photo at the end.

$55 per person · Minimum 5 guests

Ask about a date

A keepsake for the bride

Choose a design from Lorraine’s collection or tell her what the bride loves. Consider a canvas, painted wine glasses or even hand mirrors.

A game for the table

Each booking includes reusable Magic Moment Cards with “How Well Do You Know the Bride?” questions to play between brushstrokes.

What’s included

Lorraine brings the art supplies, aprons and table coverings to your venue and guides the whole group. You arrange the tables, chairs and a date that suits the wedding party.
