Fundraising paint nights.

Give supporters an evening to paint together while raising money for your cause. Your group sets the ticket price and promotes the event.

Ask about a date

How the proceeds work

After the event, Lorraine returns the ticket revenue to your group less the hosting fee. Ask about that fee when planning your ticket price. You can add refreshments, door prizes or a draw.

Who does what

Your group supplies the venue, tables and chairs and invites the painters. Lorraine brings all the materials and teaches the painting. Choose one of her designs or discuss a custom piece connected to the cause.

Groups and causes

Paint nights work for sports teams, schools, animal rescues, service clubs, charities and families fundraising for a personal cause.
