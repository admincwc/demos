Kids’ paint parties.

Let the birthday artist help choose what everyone paints. Lorraine brings the materials and guides each child at their own pace.

Ages 8 and up

Ask about a date

Choose their project

Pick a design and surface with your child ahead of time. Canvas, water bottles, hairbrushes, clips and mirrors are among the choices.

For parents

A parent or guardian stays for the full event. You provide the space, tables and chairs. Lorraine supplies the paint, brushes, aprons and table coverings.

Beyond birthdays

Plan a painting afternoon for an end-of-school celebration, sleepover or seasonal get-together. Include the group size, children’s ages and town in your inquiry.
