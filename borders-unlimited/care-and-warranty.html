Care & warranty

Your border takes 28 days to cure. Protecting it during the first few days is particularly important.

The first four hours

Cover fresh concrete with plastic if rain starts within four hours. Keep sprinklers off for at least six hours. Avoid spray, pool backwash and heavy water exposure during the first day.

Dry or sandy ground

After four hours, gently water the surrounding ground once or twice daily for several days if conditions are dry. Keep the water off the new surface. Excess cracking caused by neglected watering is excluded.

Working around the curb

Sod, irrigation and landscaping work can resume after 36 hours with care. Bridge over the curb if wheelbarrows must cross. Keep children, pets and mowers away for at least a week. Never drive over it.

Colour & surface care

Patchy colour can occur during curing. A white powdery deposit can appear after irrigation or rain. Adjust sprinkler heads, let the border dry, then lightly brush deposits with equal parts white vinegar and water.

After the first year

The curb is sealed at installation. Ask about a penetrating sealer after the first year, then roughly every three to four years as needed. Trim overgrowth periodically.

Hairline cracks & repairs

Control joints help direct cracking. A small hairline crack can be less visible than a patch, and exact colour matching is difficult. The published minimum charge for damage repairs is $1,350.

28-day materials & workmanship warranty

During the curing period, Borders Unlimited repairs or replaces curbing affected by a defect in materials or workmanship at no charge. The warranty excludes impact, roots, soil settlement and ground movement. Borders attached to walkways or driveways are excluded.

The homeowner is responsible for pre-work utility locates. Damage to underground items including sprinklers and cables is excluded. Deposits are non-refundable and transferable to another date or jobsite. Repairs and resealing are payable by cash or certified cheque before work starts.
