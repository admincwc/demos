Concrete landscape borders

We form a continuous concrete curb on your property, following the outline of an existing bed or a new layout you choose.

An edge you can mow along

A low leading edge gives a mower wheel a place to run. The concrete helps keep mulch in the bed and ordinary lawn grass on its own side, so there’s less hand trimming along the planting line.

Made to follow your yard

The extrusion machine follows curves, corners and straight runs. A galvanized cable runs through the curb as it is formed. Control joints give the concrete a place to move as it cures and the ground shifts.

Where we install borders

Frame a tree, edge a driveway or divide a commercial planting bed from turf. We can reshape existing beds during layout, before the first cut is made.
