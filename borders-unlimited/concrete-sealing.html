Concrete & stone sealing

Sealer brings out the colour in a finished surface and helps limit the watermarks and stains that can soak into bare concrete or stone.

Surfaces we seal

Stamped and plain concrete drives, interlocking stone, flagstone patios, pool surrounds and walkways can all be sealed. For a pool deck or footpath, ask about a fine SharkGrip texture additive for added traction.

Spring resealing

Customers on the resealing program receive a call in the fall to arrange spring service. We can look at your existing finish and discuss when it needs another coat.
