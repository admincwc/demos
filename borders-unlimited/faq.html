Common questions

A few practical things to know before planning a concrete border.

How long does installation take?

Most installations are finished in one day. Bill reviews access, the layout and timing when he visits to measure.

When can a border be installed?

Our usual installation window is April 15 to November 15, weather permitting. We avoid work when freezing conditions may affect curing.

Is the border made from separate blocks?

The extruder forms a continuous curb on site. The lines you see are control joints, spaced along the run to help manage cracking.

How much sod has to come out?

The strip is typically about 2½ inches deep and 10 inches wide. The base is levelled and compacted. No separate footing is required.

Can you work in an established garden?

The mixing trailer stays on the street or driveway. We bring smaller equipment into the yard through openings as narrow as 30 inches.

Will the colour wear off?

Integral colour is blended into the concrete. Sun and weather can soften the appearance over time. Sealing helps protect the surface.

How does it stand up to frost?

We use fibre mesh, galvanized cable and control joints to help accommodate movement. Concrete can develop hairline cracks as soil settles or freezes.

Will it keep grass out of a flower bed?

The curb helps contain ordinary lawn grass and mulch. Quackgrass and other spreading weeds may still cross beneath it.

How soon can I mow?

Keep mowers away for at least a week and protect the concrete for the full 28-day cure. Read the care and warranty instructions.

We’ll walk the property with you and measure it for a free written quote.
