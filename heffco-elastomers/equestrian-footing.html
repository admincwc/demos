Equestrian footing

Black rubber granules for horse tracks, riding trails and indoor or outdoor arenas.

Mixing and coverage

Allow 2,000 lb of footing per 600 square feet at one part rubber to two parts arena sand. Sold by the pound.

Base preparation

Remove grass and soil and prepare an aggregate base. Heffco recommends a natural slope of at least 1% for drainage.
