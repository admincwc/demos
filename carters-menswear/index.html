Three generations.
One Simcoe storefront.

Percy Carter opened his first clothing shop on Norfolk Street after working in menswear in Simcoe. The shop moved to 42 Norfolk Street South in the 1930s. His sons Harry and Herb carried it on, with Herb’s son Cam taking over as the third generation in 1991.
