Custom homes & cottages

A first home in Simcoe and a family cottage on Lake Erie start with different questions. Mike works through them with you, then draws the plans around your lot and the way you live.

What goes into a Ross home

A plan drawn for your lot

Meet at the Simcoe office or in your home to discuss room sizes, storage, sightlines and access. In-house CAD drawings let you review the layout before construction.

Comfort through the seasons

Insulation, heating, cooling and heat-recovery ventilation are part of the design conversation. Engineered floor systems give you more flexibility when planning larger rooms.

Decisions on site

Visit at key stages to see the spaces take shape and choose the details. If you live further away, Mike can walk you through progress with photos and video calls.

In-house design

See the cottage before you build.

A floor plan shows how rooms connect. An exterior drawing lets you see the roof, porch and windows together. Reviewing both with your builder makes it easier to decide what feels right for the site.
