Our story

A Simcoe family of builders.

Bill Ross founded William J. Ross Construction Ltd. in 1964 to build custom homes in his own community. His son Mike learned the trade at his side and now runs the business from Simcoe.

Working with Mike

Mike meets homeowners at the office on Ireland Road or at their kitchen table. He draws plans, talks through material choices and works on site. The same person who hears your ideas at the first meeting sees how they come together during construction.

Some of the families who bought an early Ross home have come back to build a second or a third.

Our service area

Simcoe to the Lake Erie shore

Ross projects extend beyond Simcoe to Port Dover, Port Ryerse, Turkey Point, Port Rowan and Long Point, as well as Delhi and Tillsonburg. That work ranges from new homes on inland lots to additions on established beach properties.

Community work

Cabinetry for a Simcoe classroom.

In 2000, Ross Construction joined Simcoe Composite School and two local partners to rebuild the Family Studies classroom. The Ross team donated its time to build the room’s new cabinetry.
