Long Point · Restoration

Old Cut Lighthouse

Working with a historical architect, the Ross team spent two years restoring the timber lighthouse and keeper’s house. The work included the windows, doors, floor planking and railing around the upper deck.

2001 SAM Awards for renovation from the Canadian and Ontario Home Builders’ Associations.

Kirkby Cottage
