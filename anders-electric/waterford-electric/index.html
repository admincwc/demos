Waterford Electric Co. Ltd.

Waterford Electric and Anders Electric joined forces in 2018.

Two local teams came together

When founder Paul Hrvoyevich retired on April 30, 2018, Waterford Electric joined Anders Electric after a year of merger planning. The combined team operates under the Anders Electric name and added home theatre installation to its residential, agricultural, commercial and industrial work.

Anders agreed to honour existing Waterford Electric warranties. Former customers can reach the Waterford office at 519-446-2922.
