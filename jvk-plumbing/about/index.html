A family plumbing shop in Simcoe.

JVK Plumbing & Heating began in 1991. John Sr., John Jr., Shirley and Andrew share a commitment to the customers who call the shop.

From our shop on Grigg Drive

Our work takes us into Norfolk County homes of very different ages. One Port Dover customer called us about a recurring leak in a house more than 160 years old. JVK redid the copper piping and replaced a circulating pump on the boiler.

At the other end of a building’s life cycle, we work with builders on new plumbing rough-ins. We also take calls for commercial and industrial properties.
