Residential plumbing

A leak under the kitchen sink and a damp spot below an upstairs bathroom can look like two different problems. Tell us what you can see, where it shows up and whether it happens every time you use a fixture.

A service call for your home

We handle residential plumbing calls from our shop in Simcoe. You can call about a visible leak, a troublesome fixture or pipework that has needed more than one repair. In an older home, it helps to know which parts of the plumbing have already been replaced.

What to mention when you call

  • The room and fixture closest to the problem.
  • Whether water appears continuously or only when a tap, toilet or appliance runs.
  • Any previous repair in the same spot, even if it was years ago.
