Hot water heating

If the boiler runs but one part of the building stays cold, that detail matters. Call JVK about hot water heating in your Simcoe or Norfolk County property.

When the heat is uneven

In a hot water heating system, heated water circulates from a boiler to radiators, baseboards or other heating zones. A single cold room and a building-wide loss of heat give the plumber different starting points. A Port Dover customer described JVK replacing the circulating pump on their boiler.

Describe the heating issue

  • Whether one room, one zone or the whole building is affected.
  • When you first noticed the change and whether it comes and goes.
  • The boiler make and model, if you can read them without opening the equipment.
