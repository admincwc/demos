New builds & rough-ins

The best time to talk through a plumbing layout is while fixture locations and framing plans can still be coordinated. JVK works with builders and homeowners planning new construction in Norfolk County.

Before the walls are closed

A rough-in puts the supply, drain and vent piping in place for the fixtures that come later. A change in shower position or kitchen layout can affect more than one pipe. Start the conversation with your floor plans, fixture selections and current construction stage.

What to have ready

  • The latest floor plans and the project address.
  • Kitchen, bathroom and laundry layouts, including any undecided fixtures.
  • Your builder’s contact information and the target framing schedule.
