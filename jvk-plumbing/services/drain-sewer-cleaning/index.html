Drain & sewer cleaning

A slow kitchen sink is a different call from several drains backing up together. JVK provides drain and sewer cleaning for properties in Simcoe and the surrounding area.

One fixture or several?

Tell us which drain showed the first sign of trouble and what happens when nearby fixtures are used. If the issue has returned after a previous cleaning, mention how long the drain worked normally. Those details help us discuss the right service call for the property.

Questions the office may ask

  • Is one sink, tub or floor drain affected, or are several fixtures involved?
  • Does water drain slowly, stand still or come back up elsewhere?
  • Has the same blockage happened before, and is an existing cleanout accessible?
