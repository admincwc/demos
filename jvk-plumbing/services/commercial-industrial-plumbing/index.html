Commercial & industrial plumbing

When a plumbing problem interrupts a shift or closes a washroom, the building layout and the people using it matter. JVK takes commercial and industrial plumbing enquiries across Norfolk County.

Planning around an operating building

A shop with one washroom has different access needs from a facility with several tenants. Tell us which part of the building is affected, whether the rest is still operating and who can provide access to the mechanical space. For planned work, we can discuss the scope with your site contact.

Details for the site call

  • The site address, entrance and on-site contact.
  • The fixtures, work areas or tenants affected.
  • Access windows, shutdown restrictions and any site-specific check-in requirements.
