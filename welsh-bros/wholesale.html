Sweet corn packs

Traditional asparagus is packed in one-pound bunches. Extra Fine and Jumbo Grillers ship in 11 lb boxes. Standard and Large ship in 28 lb boxes. Organic asparagus is available in one-pound bunches and 11 lb boxes. Ask about custom packing.
