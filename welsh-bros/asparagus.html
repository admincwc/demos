Asparagus

Early May to late June

Three springs in the making

It takes three years for new asparagus roots to produce their first harvest. We grow Millennium asparagus, a variety developed at the University of Guelph for growing conditions like ours.

From the field

During harvest we pick by hand each day, grade the spears by thickness, then hydro-cool them to draw out field heat.

At home

Choose firm green spears with tight tips. Wrap the cut ends in a damp paper towel, slip the bunch into a bag and refrigerate. Properly stored asparagus keeps for about 7 to 10 days.
