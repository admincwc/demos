Sweet corn

Late July to late September

Bi-colour sweet corn

Look for rows of white and yellow kernels tucked inside the husk. You might know this GMO-free summer favourite as Peaches and Cream.

Yellow sweet corn

Our old-fashioned yellow variety has a sweet, buttery flavour. We grow it in smaller quantities and call it “Chocolate Sunshine.”

Keeping corn fresh

Leave the husks on and refrigerate until you’re ready to cook. Under proper conditions, sweet corn keeps for 10 to 14 days.
