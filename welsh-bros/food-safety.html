Tracing each harvest

Our tracking software connects produce to its harvest location and follows it through to the point of sale. The barcode on a bag of corn links back to GPS coordinates in the field.

If a concern comes up

Those records help us identify where an affected harvest went and isolate the product quickly. For a question about produce you purchased, please contact the farm.
