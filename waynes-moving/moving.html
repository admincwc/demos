Moving & packing

Tell us what you’re moving and where it’s going. We’ll work out the crew, equipment and time your move will need.

Choosing your crew

A two-person crew suits smaller moves without heavy pieces. For an average three-bedroom house, Wayne’s recommends three movers. Mention large TVs, pianos and awkward furniture when requesting your estimate.

How local rates work

The hourly rate includes the moving van and equipment. There’s a one-hour minimum. Time starts when the van leaves our yard and ends when it returns.

Help with packing

We can pack for a move we’re handling or help you prepare for a move you’re doing yourself. Tell us which rooms or fragile items you’d like help with.

Before you get a quote

  • Pickup and delivery locations
  • Preferred moving date
  • Number of rooms and large items
  • Stairs, elevators and parking access
