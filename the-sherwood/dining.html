Sunday Brunch & Weekend Buffets

Sunday brunch runs from 10 am to 2 pm. Help yourself to eggs Benedict, breakfast hash and blueberry pancakes, then visit the dessert table for a tart or a slice of pie. Brunch is $21.95, or $19.95 for seniors.

Come back for the roast beef carvery and Lake Erie perch on the weekend dinner buffet, Friday through Sunday from 4 pm to 8 pm.
