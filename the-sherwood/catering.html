Deposits, payments and final guest count

A non-refundable deposit of $100 to $500 reserves the date. Half the estimated bill is due seven days before the event, with the balance due at its close. Confirm your guest count at least 48 hours ahead. Charges are based on the higher of the guaranteed and actual counts.
