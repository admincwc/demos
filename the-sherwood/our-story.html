The Duce Family · Brantford

Our Story

The First Booths on Colborne Street

Ernest Duce, Peter Lucek and Paul Kuchma opened the Sherwood Inn Restaurant on February 4, 1961. A coffee counter served guests from the motel next door. Behind the sliding door was a separate dining room, with a mural that Ernest painted himself.

A Brantford Sunday Tradition

The Nottingham Room opened in 1974. Sunday smorgasbord followed a year later. When Ernest’s son Dennis took over in 1979, he introduced Sunday brunch. A kitchen expansion in 1981 made room for the catering business.

The Paintings

Ernest built and decorated the restaurant. The landscapes on its walls draw on his memories of England and Canada’s East Coast.
