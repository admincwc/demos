Plan your visit

Sunday, June 27, 2027 at Shadow Lake in downtown Waterford.

Watching from shore

The racecourse follows Shadow Lake beside the Waterford Heritage Trail. Come to 77 Alice Street, across from the Antique Market and Wishbone. A lawn chair gives you a place to settle in between heats.

Get directionsAdd race day to calendar

What to bring

Bring cash for parking by donation, food vendors, festival buttons and 50/50 tickets. Nearby side streets are another parking option. Leave driveways and business parking spaces clear.

Race weekend questions

Can I come just to watch?

Yes. Spectators are welcome all day on Sunday, June 27. The organizers suggest allowing 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Will there be food?

Food vendors are on site and downtown restaurants are nearby. Some vendors accept cash only. Leave barbecues and alcohol at home.

What happens if it rains?

Racing goes ahead in rain. Thunder or lightning can delay or suspend races. There is no alternate race date.

Can our team set up a tent?

Each team can bring one canopy up to 10 by 10 feet. The organizers cannot reserve a pitch. Bring your own chairs, table and shade.

What should paddlers wear?

Wear secure footwear for the docks and boat. Bring a change of clothes, a towel and protection for the weather. Leave valuables ashore.

Race weekend

Community crews practise on Saturday afternoon. Racing takes place on Sunday.
