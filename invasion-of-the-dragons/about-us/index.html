About the festival

A weekend of dragon boat racing that keeps its fundraising close to home.

How it began

The Cancer Support & Resource Program of Norfolk organized the first Invasion of the Dragons in 2015. The Rotary Club of Norfolk Sunrise joined as a festival partner in 2023. The event raises funds for the work of both organizations in Norfolk County.

Cancer Support & Resource Program of Norfolk

This volunteer-driven organization helps residents and families affected by cancer. Its work includes financial assistance, peer and telephone support, referrals and chances for families to take a break during treatment.

Visit the Cancer Support & Resource Program

Rotary Club of Norfolk Sunrise

Chartered in 1996, the club brings Norfolk residents together for community service. Members work alongside CSRP volunteers, race officials and local sponsors to stage the weekend.

Visit the Rotary Club of Norfolk Sunrise

The cancer ceremony

Crews gather on the lake to honour people living with cancer, survivors and loved ones who have died. The ceremony is part of the festival’s history and appears in the photo archives.
