Weekend schedule

Practice on Saturday afternoon. Race day on Sunday, June 27, 2027.

Saturday, June 26

Afternoon

Team practices

Captains arrange a one-hour slot with the registration coordinator. Practice is required for community and novice crews and optional for competitive teams.

Sunday, June 27

These are the organizers’ approximate times. Captains should check their assigned heats with the race officials.

7:30 a.m.

Team setup

Set up your team area and one canopy by the lake.

Around 8 a.m.

Registration and captains’ briefing

Check in at the registration tent and meet the Alkame officials.

Around 8:30 a.m.

Racing begins

Three boats race each 250-metre heat.

Afternoon

Division finals

First-place crews receive medals after their division final. Plan to stay until about 4 p.m.
